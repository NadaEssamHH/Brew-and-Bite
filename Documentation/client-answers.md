## 8. Features (one block per feature)

### Menu Browsing & Discovery
- [C] Needed: yes. Why: reduce wait/confusion seeing what's available.
- [C] Categories: Signature Brews, Artisan Bakery, Brunch & Bowls, Cold Beverages, Sweet Treats.
- [C] Item details shown: name, price, description, origin/roast notes, badges ("Bestseller," "Freshly Baked," "Ceremonial Grade"), sold-out/availability status.
- [C] Search: by name or ingredient.
- [C] Filters: dietary tags (dairy-free, gluten-free, and similar), and service type (pickup vs. delivery).

### Customization
- [C] Coffee/drinks: milk choice (whole, oat, almond), extra espresso shot, roast type (light/medium/dark) where relevant.
- [C] Food: heating option, "warm upon request."
- [C] Trigger: customer forgetting to specify milk type was cited as a recurring real problem.

### Cart & Order Summary
- [C] Persistent/sticky summary showing item count, table/order type, and subtotal — example given: "3 items in order, Table #8, 385 EGP."
- [C] Add, remove, adjust quantity before checkout.

### Checkout
- [C] Fulfillment choice: Table Pickup (requires table number) or Instant Delivery (requires name, phone number, delivery address).
- [C] Optional order note field (dietary requests/delivery instructions).
- [C] Guest checkout, no account required.
- [C] Review step before submission.
- [D] Order hand-off channel: client prefers WhatsApp/confirmation message with pre-filled details but is open to direct on-site submission — "I'm open to that too — I mainly care that the details arrive complete and clear." **Decision needed with client.**
- [C] No online payment collection; payment handled outside the system as today.

### Admin / Management
- [C] Add, edit, remove menu items.
- [C] Mark items sold out / toggle availability.
- [C] Update daily specials.
- [C] Update prices and descriptions.
- [A] Single admin user (Karim) for now; no confirmed multi-role access.
